SUGMAD Intelligence

Helping you understand the operating environment

Threat-based analysis for the Gulf of Guinea, the Niger Delta and Nigeria’s ports and inland routes.

We offer tailored intelligence products that help clients manage uncertainty. Drawing on local sources and in-house geospatial analysis, we use threat-based information to forecast when and how a threat is likely to be realised. Understanding local dynamics allows us to evidence a proportionate response to challenges — and to identify where the operating picture is better than the headlines suggest.

Our Products

Pre-Deployment Risk Assessment

A written assessment of a specific port call, route or site before you commit, covering threat actors, incident history and recommended mitigations.

Recurring Threat Reporting

Scheduled reporting on the maritime and onshore threat picture across your areas of operation, with change-of-posture alerts between editions.

Geospatial Analysis & Incident Mapping

Spatial analysis of incident history, approach routes and facility exposure, delivered as annotated mapping alongside written assessment.

Facility Vulnerability Assessment

On-site survey of physical security, procedures and personnel, with a prioritised and costed remediation plan.

Route & Journey Risk Planning

Assessment of specific road and waterway movements, with journey management procedures and defined check-in protocols.

Analyst Support

Direct access to the analyst who wrote your assessment, for follow-up questions throughout the engagement period.
